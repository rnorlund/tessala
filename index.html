Second image (figure B)
Angel-&-Devil style: one tile shape, two fillings. What image? Not a random photo — a figure drawn to fill the tile shape. Easiest: hit ⬇ tile shape, paint your creature inside that outline, then load one version as the main image (set A) and another as 2nd figure (B) (the alternate cells). Transparent PNG is best; a white background works if you enable knock out background. You can also Shift-drop a file to set B.
Split into two shapes
Sculpt the cut (with the Shape tool): drag the cyan dots, click the cut line to add a point, right-click a point to remove. The endpoints snap to the tile edge. Two different shapes result (A = gold, B = purple); export each, fill it, load as image (→ A) and 2nd figure (→ B).
⚠ On rotational patterns (3-fold / 4-fold / 6-fold) the two halves get spun around each centre into a pinwheel, not two separate figures. For two clean, readable shapes use Glide, Square, Hexagon, or 2-fold.
Transmutation
Smooth morph (bird→fish): make figure B (e.g. fit a fish) → 📌 set shape B (grabs its shape + image) → make figure A (fit a bird) → turn on 🌅 Transmutation. A warps smoothly into B down the image. Leave B unset for figure-ground/single-motif.

For a real shape change, turn on ✂ Split first — then shape A (figure) rules the top and dissolves into the sky, while the different shape B emerges from the water below (figure-ground swap, clean interlock). Without split it's a single motif dissolving into the background.

✍️ What should it be?
Compact, bold subjects tile best (fox, owl, rhino, whale, turtle, bird). Long thin things fit worse.
Glide = clean offset rows of whole figures (the reference look). Auto/rot3/rot4 = intricate interlocks (busier).
Gap-free redraws the figure to fill every pixel (dense interlock). Keep my drawing places your exact clean drawing into a creature-shaped tile — crisp, recognisable whole figures (best for clean results; pair with Glide for reference-style offset rows).
